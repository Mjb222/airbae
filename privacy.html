Legal

Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2025

1. Who We Are

AirBae is a behavioral support app designed to help people quit smoking and vaping. We are committed to protecting your personal data and being transparent about how we use it.

Contact: airbaeapp@gmail.com

2. What Data We Collect

When you join our waiting list or use our app, we may collect:

  • Your email address and first name (via our waitlist form)
  • Usage data within the app (e.g. smoking/vaping logs, craving entries)
  • Device information (e.g. operating system, app version)
  • Anonymised behavioral data to improve the app experience

We do not collect sensitive health data beyond what you voluntarily enter into the app.

3. How We Use Your Data

We use your data to:

  • Send you updates, early access notifications, and quit-support tips (waitlist only)
  • Operate and improve the AirBae app and its features
  • Conduct anonymised research to validate the effectiveness of our program
  • Comply with our legal obligations

We will never sell your personal data to third parties.

4. Legal Basis (GDPR)

We process your data on the following legal bases:

  • Consent — for marketing emails and waitlist sign-ups
  • Legitimate interest — for app improvement and analytics
  • Contract performance — when you use the app as a registered user

5. Third-Party Services

We use the following third-party services:

  • Kit (ConvertKit) — for email list management. Their privacy policy applies to data collected via our signup form: kit.com/privacy
  • Apple TestFlight — for beta app distribution. Apple's privacy policy applies.

6. Data Retention

We retain your email address for as long as you remain subscribed to our list. You can unsubscribe at any time using the link at the bottom of any email we send.

App usage data is retained for the duration of your account and deleted upon request.

7. Your Rights

Under GDPR, you have the right to:

  • Access the personal data we hold about you
  • Request correction or deletion of your data
  • Object to or restrict how we process your data
  • Request data portability
  • Withdraw consent at any time

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at airbaeapp@gmail.com.

8. Contact & Complaints

If you have any questions about this policy, please contact us at airbaeapp@gmail.com.

You also have the right to lodge a complaint with your local data protection authority.
