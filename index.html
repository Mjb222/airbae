The Problem

The current tools are failing millions.

Existing cessation apps treat addiction as a willpower problem — they count cigarettes and show timers. But addiction is neurological, emotional, and social. Without addressing all three, relapse is inevitable.

The aim of this study is to demonstrate that our app improves your well-being and health behavior. To achieve this, we strengthen your health literacy, your self-efficacy, and your motivation.
