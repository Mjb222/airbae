Data Protection

Last updated: March 2025

Our Commitment

At AirBae, protecting your data is not just a legal obligation — it is a core part of how we build trust with our users. We process only the data we truly need, and we are transparent about everything we do with it.

We will never sell, rent, or trade your personal data with third parties for commercial purposes.

GDPR Compliance

AirBae complies with the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and applicable European data protection law. As a user, you have full rights over your data at all times.

  • Right to access your data
  • Right to correct inaccurate data
  • Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")
  • Right to restrict processing
  • Right to data portability
  • Right to object to processing

To exercise any of these rights, contact us at airbaeapp@gmail.com and we will respond within 30 days.

Data We Collect & Why

  • Email address & name — to send you waitlist updates and early access notifications (with your consent)
  • App usage data — to personalise your quit journey and improve the app
  • Anonymised behavioral data — for research purposes only, never linked to your identity

Data Security

We use industry-standard security measures to protect your data, including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS) and secure third-party infrastructure.

Our email service is managed by Kit (ConvertKit), which is GDPR-compliant. No payment data is stored by AirBae.

Cookies

Our landing page does not use tracking cookies or advertising pixels. The only external scripts loaded are our Kit email form embed and Google Fonts for typography. No behavioral tracking occurs on this website.
